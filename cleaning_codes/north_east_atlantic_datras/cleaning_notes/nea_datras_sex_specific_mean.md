# NEA DATRAS: sex-specific mean length at age

The sex ratio comes from the pooled sample of survey*species*year*quarter, within each length bin (of `len_freq`). Note this isn't calculated at the haul level (because in a haul, we may have to few samples to calulcate reliable sex-ratios by length bin, whereas at the survey*species*year*quarter level, 92.2% of the aged fish sit in bins with more than 10 aged fish).
